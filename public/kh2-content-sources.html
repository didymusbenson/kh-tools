KH2 guide sources and attribution

Additional factual comparisons use Freedom-Kona’s Final Mix guide, other individually cited GameFAQs authors, Quest Guide, 30somethinggaming, Steam and Exophase. Per-record provenance and edition limitations are in the audit.

Other factual references include Kingdom Hearts Wiki enemy and item reward tables, KHGuides, Gamer Guides, WalkthroughWizard, and the post-game enemy census.

## What the user’s challenge changed

The earlier review treated a known target or a named source as sufficient too often. “Aim above the threshold” solves an equality disagreement, but it does not teach a player how to score. “Consult this other record” was also weak when the runtime did not create that link. The correction checks the actions actually visible in the app rather than defending the deferral count.

Current counts: **20 resolved / 13 partial / 2 conflicted evidence families**; **20 resolved / 13 deferred / 2 open research dispositions**. Family counts are coarse: KH3-018 and KH3-020 still have deferred probability/equality details, but their missing play methods were reopened, researched and completed. ## Forest Clasp: the previous conflict was overstated

The [world reward table](https://www.khwiki.com/Game:Kingdom_of_Corona) says the opportunity ends when Rapunzel leaves the party. It never says her final departure. The [narrative walkthrough](https://www.gamerguides.com/kingdom-hearts-iii/guide/walkthrough/kingdom-hearts-iii/kingdom-of-corona) places her departure directly after the Shore reward scene. The [item page](https://www.khwiki.com/Forest_Clasp) explicitly says the Clasp cannot be obtained after reaching Shore, and [GosuNoob’s demonstration description](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zVHLAPwzBdw) identifies sliding to Shore as the reward step at 6:36.

These descriptions agree on the supported practical boundary. No post-Shore recovery was found; the correct advice is to finish all four tasks before that scene, keep a pre-Shore save and reload it if necessary. Festival Dance afterward is not a recovery route. A new run obtains the item on the new run, not retroactively on the old file. KH3-005 is now **resolved**, with the former contradictory interpretation preserved as history. No retail event-flag experiment or video-frame inspection is claimed.

## Player-value test and method

For each residual, ask whether its absence prevents progressing, collecting the item, or accomplishing the achievement. Check the canonical instructions rather than inferring sufficiency from a record count. Research a missing action, path or safety condition; defer a full coordinate census, earliest-access graph, probability model, timer or platform matrix when a supported practical route already meets the goal. Do not defer a concrete irreversible eligibility problem simply because a clean future attempt would avoid it.

This pass reviewed the entire KH3 ledger and canonical JSON, reused previously inspected evidence with its provenance, and reopened public sources where a practical gap remained. It did not repeat the 335-item or 59-ingredient source census: those landmark routes were already integrated.

## First-pass investigation, evidence and remaining blockers

The following narrative records the first pass. The [complete residual continuation](research-continuation-2026-10-01.md) supersedes its API-null and three-base-duration statements: 47 keys are now mapped, and Dumbo timing is sourced. The current table above incorporates those results.

### COM-001 — Moogle distributions

Reopened [Moogle Shop](https://www.khwiki.com/Moogle_Shop), the [Prima Moogle chapter](https://primagames.com/eguides/kingdom-hearts-hd-15-remix-eguide/rcom-essentials/how-to-play/moogle-shops), [falconesque](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps2/954016-kingdom-hearts-rechain-of-memories/faqs/56913), [FAQ 55459](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps2/954016-kingdom-hearts-rechain-of-memories/faqs/55459) and [Fandom Moogle Shop](https://kingdomhearts.fandom.com/wiki/Moogle_Shop). The accessible wiki table mixes original prices (270/300); it supplies no complete HD value/Premium distribution. Prima/GameFAQs direct requests returned 403; Fandom returned 402. FAQ 55459 was subsequently recovered through an indexed web retrieval reference: its Moogle/Premium room descriptions are generic, with no full HD probability table. Previously rejected GBA probability tables remain rejected. Neither missing probability cells nor locked-card renormalization were inferred from roster size. This is an evidence-access/edition problem, not an unattempted transcription.

### COM-003–005 — All farms, footnotes and mushrooms

Reread the explicitly remake-selected infoboxes linked by all 30 `enemy-card-farms.json` records and their world/strategy text. [Shadow](https://www.khwiki.com/Shadow) is present in Atlantica **only through Bottomless Darkness**; [Soldier](https://www.khwiki.com/Soldier) appears in Neverland **only when summoned by Crescendo**. Its original CoM Destiny Islands appearance is excluded. Both exceptions now appear in canonical acquisition text and generated drop locations. The source lists do not enumerate every generated battle composition/reset loop, so COM-003 remains partial.

[White Mushroom](https://www.khwiki.com/White_Mushroom): use White Room and satisfy three requested Fire/Blizzard/Thunder gestures; [Warp](https://www.khwiki.com/Warp) is also eligible, and its no-EXP effect does not suppress map/enemy-card drops. [Black Fungus](https://www.khwiki.com/Black_Fungus): use Black Room and defeat it; striking its field model postpones its temporary invulnerability. Do not import KH1 combo-finish requirements. The [Enemy Card](https://www.khwiki.com/Enemy_Card) last-enemy selection rule still applies, and Darkness room boosts do not apply to these exclusive rooms. These are now complete source-backed success instructions; exact generated reset routes remain COM-003.

The independently hosted [Gamergirl guide](https://gamergirlguides.com/2023/10/khcom-enemycards) corroborates the generic 2–4% range, last-enemy rule and 2.5 multiplier but mixes legacy bonus context and does not supply all 30 HD rows. Multiple KHWiki pages do not provide source independence. COM-005 therefore remains a narrow corroboration limitation; no game-play measurement is demanded.

### COM-013–014 — Progression and combat catalog

[Level](https://www.khwiki.com/Level) and [Stats](https://www.khwiki.com/Stats) supply the full remake curve: level 2 costs 25 EXP; levels 3–99 cost `4 × level²`; both campaigns cap at 99. `progression.json` retains all 99 level rows with cumulative totals. Sora starts at 80 HP/275 CP, gaining 15/25 to caps 560/1625; AP is fixed at 10. Riku starts at 80 HP/10 AP/8 DP, gaining 15/1/2 to caps 560/30/99. AP choices unlock at level 2 and every three levels through 59; deferred choices persist. Sora's missed sleights remain in fixed learn order, and only choosing HP heals on a remake level-up. DP upgrade availability is distinct from the Dark Mode activation meter.

Every ordinary enemy and available boss/form/body KHRECOM stat table was extracted into `combat-reference.json`: **59 records / 379 floor rows**, including 43 unambiguous card-count/time duel pairs. Sources include the 30 farm pages, [Darkball](https://www.khwiki.com/Darkball), [Marluxia](https://www.khwiki.com/Game:Marluxia), [Riku Replica](https://www.khwiki.com/Game:Riku_Replica), [Ursula](https://www.khwiki.com/Game:Ursula), [Zexion](https://www.khwiki.com/Game:Zexion) and every record's explicit source URL. Source phase paths distinguish bodies/limbs/forms; inactive black-on-black campaign floor labels become null. Numeric stats/resistances and applicable explicit timers propagate into enemy-card reference notes with their combat-source provenance.

Remaining exact problems: the source's Ursula body/tentacle B11F row conflicts with Riku's Atlantica floor group, so its Riku floor is null with the conflicting label preserved; no guessed B5F is substituted. Replica's five-card 6-second and seven-card 8-second alternatives lack a certified encounter mapping. Marluxia Bit AP is `?` in the source. Early Ansem tutorial values are not identified by the final-boss table. All 24 explicit remake boss deck tables from the inspected pages are also extracted (111 card-family rows), including six Replica encounters and three distinct Marluxia forms. They supply multiplicities by value, not ordered decks. Zexion’s magic and stolen-Soul-Eater phases remain separate. Source tables have no ordered deck state or complete frame data; full encounter tactics/routes and unlisted deck variants remain incomplete. This extraction closes numeric table absence, not the entire broader combat finding.

Modern use retains the existing edition-selected remake values; no fresh claim about unchanged Steam engine code is made.

## COM-007 — concrete stock cases

- [Page 388](https://www.kh13.com/gallery/image/18553-kh2fm_ultimania_388/): pure Attack recipes depend on their identity constraints and sum, with ingredient order immaterial. Their structured `ordered` flag is now false. Magic/summon/friend recipes retain specified ordering unless a documented alternative applies.
- [Page 392](https://www.kh13.com/gallery/image/18566-kh2fm_ultimania_392/): Trinity Limit accepts the Attack/Donald/Goofy permutations **except Goofy → Donald → Attack**, which selects Wild Crush. The existing five alternatives are corroborated, not expanded to six.
- [Page 394](https://www.kh13.com/gallery/image/18600-kh2fm_ultimania_394/): a spare non-Cure card **before Cure → Cure** preserves both Cures for normal reload. A third Cure instead matches Curaga. This extends the existing appended-Item example without claiming arbitrary middle insertion.
- [Page 402](https://www.kh13.com/gallery/image/18621-kh2fm_ultimania_402/): Goofy → Attack can be followed by another card to increase stock value while using Goofy Smash. [Page 403](https://www.kh13.com/gallery/image/18615-kh2fm_ultimania_403/) supplies the two-card base recipe and ordered Wild Crush recipe.
- [Pages 268–269](https://www.kh13.com/gallery/image/18519-kh2fm_ultimania_269/): learned status and correct matching matter; an extra card can accompany a two-card technique, and the displayed move name is the practical in-game check. This does not enumerate all overlapping subsequence/tie rules.

The [existing Lua connector](https://github.com/gaithern/KH-RECOM-AP-LUA/blob/d5e5c06bb8dd803ee153d2ba47a862fc773c9901/recomAPConnector.lua) was cloned and inspected at the same pinned commit: learned-flag packing/writes and level-up replacements are present, but no stock-recipe evaluator was found there. A mod's flag allocation is not vanilla achievement or matching logic. `thirdCardPrecedence` deliberately remains null, and no general-purpose solver was added.

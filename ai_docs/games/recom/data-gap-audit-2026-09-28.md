The 24 Room of Rewards entries display costs for every eligible floor; ordinary Bounty rooms do not consume Key to Rewards. Added order for all 17 named Bounties and readable post-clear prerequisites.

Gold/Platinum entries now provide sequential acquisition and effects. Achievement copy distinguishes accumulated battle cards from a held Moogle Point balance.

## COM-001: primary evidence recovered

The decisive lead was the edition-separated [KHWiki editor draft](https://www.khwiki.com/User:TheSilentHero/Moogle_Shop), checked against original Japanese **Kingdom Hearts II Final Mix+ Ultimania**, Re:CoM chapter, [page 382](https://www.kh13.com/gallery/image/18575-kh2fm_ultimania_382/) and [page 383](https://www.kh13.com/gallery/image/18552-kh2fm_ultimania_383/). Both scanned pages were visually inspected; hashes of downloaded evidence are in `source-manifest.json`. Scans are not redistributed in the repository.

Page 382 supplies value distributions and the Premium rate, and expressly excludes locked identities. Page 383 supplies the attack/magic/item identity tables and says assorted cards choose among the three categories with equal one-third probability. All identity pools and value distributions sum to 100. The eight pre-existing magic/item pools agree with the scan. Item cards cannot be Premium; the assorted Premium field applies only to its eligible attack/magic selections, not to every draw.

The original PS2 scan contains Monochrome, Follow the Wind, Hidden Dragon and Photon Debugger. The editor draft maps those slots to HD Total Eclipse, Midnight Roar, Maverick Flare and Two Become One, respectively. Original names were not added to the HD roster. The scan independently supports the underlying table, but the HD mapping remains community-reference evidence; neither source is a fresh Steam measurement.

Data lives in `moogle-packs.json` (`cardRates`, `valueOdds`, conditional `premiumFrequency`, exact fractional `categorySelection`) and each attack card's `shopRates`. Assorted records reference the corresponding tier/category pools instead of rounding one-third to 33% or duplicating a flattened distribution. Runtime shop and attack entries expose these facts.

**Boundary:** exclusion does not establish how the game handles a roll for a locked identity. We did not infer proportional renormalization, redraw order, fallback cards, or independent joint probabilities. Unlocking every card is the stated scope for the published identity percentages. The prior Prima table remains rejected for this purpose because it conflicts with edition-selected remake values; it was not silently rehabilitated.

## COM-002: hallway scope and battle state

Two Japanese community references explicitly connect the B1F Castle Oblivion deck to castle exit halls: [Disney Database](https://w.atwiki.jp/wrtb/pages/13395.html) and [KH series world reference](https://wikiwiki.jp/kh_series/ワールド/【忘却の城】). Their independence is not established. The latter also distinguishes the remake's changed battle arenas and Castle deck from the GBA original. Combined with the already extracted [KHRECOM Castle table](https://www.khwiki.com/Game:Castle_Oblivion), this supports using the existing 26 Soul Eater cards plus value-8 Hi-Potion for ordinary corridor boss fights, including Vexen, first Replica and Lexaeus. No replacement sequence was invented.

This corridor rule does not move every humanoid boss into the Castle deck: Zexion's world encounter remains on the Destiny Islands reference, and the second Replica encounter remains in Twilight Town. The early Ansem tutorial remains separately qualified rather than certified from a general hallway statement.

[Dark Mode](https://www.khwiki.com/Dark_Mode) explains a distinct battle-state change: transformation reloads the deck and removes Item cards until the reload after returning to normal. Its Zexion exception is permanent Dark Mode. This is surfaced on the world deck entries so the preset is not mistaken for a promise that every listed card is always currently selectable.

The [PS4 Beginner runner guide](https://www.speedrun.com/khrecom/guides/bzl3k) describes using earned Dragon Maleficent/Jafar cards in multiple later encounters, supporting practical retention. It does not enumerate every retained card and its insertion order at every encounter, so it cannot certify a complete override/state matrix. Existing acquisition conditions and the retained-card union are preserved.

## COM-003: practical encounters and a corrected confidence claim

The [BlankRange RNG guide](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps2/954016-kingdom-hearts-rechain-of-memories/faqs/81607) transcribes DERIKUF routes; it is not independent corroboration of those videos. Its encounter instructions supply these targeted carrier leads:

| Target | World | Field enemy in the reported Sleeping Darkness route |
|---|---|---|
| Blue Rhapsody | Traverse Town | Soldier |
| Air Soldier | Monstro | Large Body |
| Search Ghost | Monstro | Shadow |
| Pirate | Neverland | Shadow |
| Air Pirate | Neverland | Darkball |
| Defender / Wyvern | Hollow Bastion | Shadow; composition varies |
| Neoshadow | Castle Oblivion | Red Nocturne; target is in a later wave |

These are encounter leads, not exhaustive room spawn tables or guaranteed formations. The three existing detailed optional recipes remain intact. The [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2552430/discussions/0/4515505458306858761/) supports the broader retry method, not a universal one-attempt or ten-attempt guarantee.

[Barrel Spider](https://www.khwiki.com/Barrel_Spider) distinguishes remake prop persistence: merely leaving the room does not restore destroyed barrels. Preserve a save with barrels intact or synthesize another room. Self-destruction loses the card prize, so defeat the target before it explodes. This practical exception is now attached to the farm.

The [Soldier page](https://www.khwiki.com/Soldier) really does attach a Neverland/Crescendo-only footnote to its **Re:CoM** infobox, as well as the original-edition table. However, [Crescendo's remake ability](https://www.khwiki.com/Crescendo) summons species already present in the battle, whereas its GBA ability specifically summons Soldiers. Those statements do not establish a reliable Neverland Soldier encounter. The earlier “both footnotes resolved” claim was too strong. Canonical conflict evidence is preserved; player guidance recommends Traverse Town, Wonderland or Twilight Town. We do not assert that Neverland Soldiers are impossible.

The older [falconesque guide](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps2/954016-kingdom-hearts-rechain-of-memories/faqs/56913) describes escape-based encounter rerolls and differences between visible and approach-spawned field enemies. This was not added as the default retry path: escaping conflicts with a No Escape run, and the already integrated save/reload route avoids that conflict. No claim about a guaranteed number of attempts or universal prop reset was added.

## Leads exhausted and next evidence needed

- Prima's mixed probability table and anecdotal Moogle RNG indexing do not reveal vanilla locked-pool handling. The original Ultimania pages substantially improve coverage, but do not specify the missing algorithm.
- The public Archipelago Lua connector at `gaithern/KH-RECOM-AP-LUA`, commit `d5e5c06bb8dd803ee153d2ba47a862fc773c9901`, edits randomizer state and initial cards; inspected code did not supply vanilla Moogle selection, Riku preset tables or enemy formations.
- World and boss guides establish locations, tactics and the general retention rule, but do not contain a complete ordered inventory capture for every scripted Riku state.
- Thirty enemy infoboxes give world/rate lists; a transcribed RNG route is not an exhaustive formation database. Full closure needs a remake formation table or captured encounter coverage, and a direct test of the disputed summon case.

The useful next targets are therefore specific: a vanilla Moogle routine or documented locked-pool experiment; early Ansem and retained-card menu captures; and remake formation data. Repeating generic walkthrough searches is unlikely to resolve these residuals.

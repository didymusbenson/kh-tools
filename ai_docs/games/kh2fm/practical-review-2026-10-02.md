## Deferred research and reasoning

### KH2-003

- **Player goal:** Collect the three remaining upper-Mineshaft crowns and finish Sunset.
- **Already sufficient / integrated now:** Each of Sunset 32/36/40 gives the Engine Chamber/Magic Boost/white-orb starting point and complete Glide LV3 pipe sweep to the far landing. All three physical crowns and the solved Sunset board are covered.
- **Deferred precision:** Independent binding of each pipe/landing to board slots 32, 36 and 40; not the three-crown route itself.
- **Why defer:** The shared sweep collects the entire small group. Certifying a per-pipe slot would shorten an already localized search, but is not needed to obtain the crowns or finish the board. Existing uncertainty is retained.
- **Reopen when:** A player cannot find a remaining crown with the sweep, or a requested missing-slot-only locator needs exact mapping.

### KH2-006

- **Player goal:** Find and buy equipment, including avoiding Dark Anklet missability.
- **Already sufficient / integrated now:** All 131 equipment records have acquisition text; named vendor/loot/reward/recipe paths are present. Dark Anklet specifically explains visiting Disney Castle Mogjiro before the Lingering Will portal cutoff and the later Twilight Town/Radiant Garden stock sharing.
- **Deferred precision:** Every retail shop stock event flag, intermediate story-phase and room-spawn predicate, plus Steam binary parity.
- **Why defer:** No equipment record is left without an acquisition route. Exhaustive early-access vendor availability would support an earliest-purchase optimizer that the current journal does not offer; it is not required for the listed completion routes. The known permanent cutoff remains prominent.
- **Reopen when:** An actual listed vendor route fails in an identified story state, or an earliest-purchase planner is approved.

### KH2-019

- **Player goal:** Meet Mushroom Journal targets and obtain challenge rewards.
- **Already sufficient / integrated now:** All twelve scored challenges have targets, rank/reward tables and strategies. The script-derived HP-floor warning and XII warp-specific Thunder protection are integrated. XIII is a reward event after the first twelve, with Look Up and Ready, Go! actions.
- **Deferred precision:** Complete Mushroom AI control-flow and external initialization, XIII combat-sheet mapping, every effective phase stat and Steam byte parity.
- **Why defer:** The missing internal model does not change the supplied challenge route or reward action. XIII does not require defeating a hypothetical HP bar. A complete combat simulator or frame-perfect optimizer is beyond this acquisition guide.
- **Reopen when:** A published tactic fails because of a specific phase rule or a separately requested simulator needs exact effective values.

# DDD HD — future improvements

**Corrected scope:14 wholly deferred families, with two formerly deferred families reopened and one resolved after an adapter correction.** The initial count of17 was too broad. Missing practical actions/source conflicts are not removed simply because another provider exists. See [critical re-audit](critical-reaudit-2026-10-02.md) and the [full review](practical-review-2026-10-02.md).

The following precision is optional for the current documented routes; no schedule or release gate is assigned. Unknown facts remain unknown. A concrete player problem reopens the relevant question.

## DDD-001

**Goal:** Collect the 438 HD treasures and their rewards.

**Current route:** All 438 item/character/Reports identities have pickup landmarks, 51 additional source notes, and 11 Grid action improvements. Known exceptional access instructions remain on their specific entries.

**Deferred:** Exhaustive earliest-story-event, minimum-movement and returnability fields for every chest; 437 route rows lack an explicit earliest-access statement.

**Why:** The current checklist supports finding each pickup, and no specific remaining inaccessible chest has been identified. An exhaustive access matrix would support a progression-gating feature that is explicitly outside the current app, rather than fix a known failed collection route.

**Reopen:** A player cannot reach a listed chest using its route, or a documented missable/restricted-access exception is found.

## DDD-005

**Goal:** Create the HD-only Spirits and unlock their useful disposition branches.

**Current route:** Recipes, costs, board rewards and known interactions are present. Five missing direct transitions now have explicit two-step alternatives through known cells. A sourced treat-color route also avoids petting precision.

**Deferred:** 15 base-stat values for Beatalike/Catanuki/Tubguin Ace and five direct-interaction body-part cells.

**Why:** Base-stat comparison is an optimization issue, not a prerequisite to creation or board rewards. The five missing interactions are not unreachable dispositions: all can be reached through sourced alternatives. Unknown cells remain unknown.

**Reopen:** A stat calculator is approved, an actual combat choice depends on missing values, or a documented interaction detour fails.

## DDD-007

**Goal:** Create useful ranks, earn Golden Egg and train Spirits for board level gates.

**Current route:** Material rank thresholds, the weaker-ingredient rule and donation bonuses are documented. Golden Egg now has a PS4 HD route: custom-create with 10 Intrepid Figments + 8 Vibrant Figments for either Tama Sheep or Pricklemane at ★. Both base-B outcomes receive +2 ranks from doubling both ingredients; no weather or specific-breed guarantee is needed. Use the preview and train afterward; the malformed starting-level table and weather probability arithmetic are not required.

**Deferred:** Two malformed starting-level cells and relative-versus-percentage-point interpretation of Risky Winds odds.

**Why:** The concrete ★-rank achievement route is supplied and does not depend on uncertain weather arithmetic. Exact initial-level/weather optimization remains unnecessary for creating and leveling a breed; unknown arithmetic stays out of calculations.

**Reopen:** A supported route needs a precise starting-level threshold or a weather/expected-cost optimizer is approved.

## DDD-009

**Goal:** Obtain every Dream Piece and gather ingredients for Spirit creation.

**Current route:** All 37 materials have sourced enemy/portal routes and chest/shop joins. Rare and ordinary forms stay separate; newly added portal landmarks also propagate into material sources. The re-audit added a repeatable PS4-documented Riku TWTNW Special3 farming loop sufficient to gather the five Brilliant Fantasies used by the three marked formulas, plus the Sora Wild Fantasy counterpart.

**Deferred:** Every ordinary enemy room/spawn loop, exact expiration quantities, earliest possible replay gate and exhaustive payout transitions outside the documented Brilliant/Wild repeat-farm route.

**Why:** Room-by-room optimal farming and expiration quantities remain optional after adding an actual rotating repeat-farm action for the scarce materials. The former existence-of-routes-only rationale was insufficient; the new loop tells players how to obtain more than their first clear.

**Reopen:** A required material cannot be obtained by its listed routes, or a demonstrated farm needs room-level/repeat-reward correction.

## DDD-011

**Goal:** Acquire commands and use Strike Raid in combat.

**Current route:** All 124 acquisition/default routes, slots/uses and 78 of 79 applicable reloads are documented. Strike Raid acquisition is unaffected; its recharge indicator supplies the actionable readiness cue. Quick Blitz is already corrected to 100 munny (80 on sale).

**Deferred:** Strike Raid exact base reload, 22 versus 24 seconds.

**Why:** The two-second disagreement does not prevent collection or ordinary use. The app has no certified frame/timing optimizer that needs a fabricated single value.

**Reopen:** A cooldown simulator or exact combat rotation is approved, or a source-labelled HD measurement resolves the discrepancy.

## DDD-013

**Goal:** Activate required Links and complete Link-related goals.

**Current route:** All 43 Link entries, provider/pair rules and gauge requirements are present. Entries now direct Steam players to their configured on-screen action prompts and visible gauge.

**Deferred:** A fixed keyboard/controller binding crosswalk and every exact Link duration.

**Why:** Bindings can be remapped and the game shows the current prompt and gauge. Recording every default device label and timing constant is not necessary to activate a documented pair.

**Reopen:** A specific Link cannot be executed from its prompts or a control scheme requires missing action guidance.

## DDD-014

**Goal:** Find and clear all 78 Specials and 11 Secrets for End of Pain, Unbound and portal achievements.

**Current route:** All character/world/area/forecast/unlock rows remain. Added 22 HD location qualifiers for ambiguous levels, corners and duplicate areas, plus a forecast/Reports/expanded-map hunting method. **Reopen:** A particular required portal still cannot be located, or a listed reward route fails on a repeat.

## DDD-015

**Goal:** Use built-in Battle/Friendship portals and complete the seven bonus-objective types.

**Current route:** 257 built-in identities cover all 316 configurations; area/forecast/objective rows are searchable. The expanded map locates active portals, and Brave Challengers explicitly lists seven types. Brave Challengers now names seven exact Special Portal examples, one for each bonus type, with character, world, area, forecast and unlock condition.

**Deferred:** Exact landmarks for every built-in portal and certification of source-number suffixes as Journal numbering.

**Why:** The seven required bonus goals now have concrete places to attempt them. Detailed routes to every optional built-in portal and cosmetic source numbering can remain deferred; a list of objective names alone was not the sufficiency test.

**Reopen:** A specific needed bonus type cannot be found or a duplicate identity causes incorrect user progress.

## DDD-017

**Goal:** Unlock Secret Cup, earn Sweet Dreams and complete Flick Rush rank/medal goals.

**Current route:** Ten cups, 27 lineups, all available published scoring/prize tables and 19 Rush milestones exist. Conservative access advice now says clear the other cups and reach LV17, satisfying both disputed thresholds; actual result screens confirm rank/medals.

**Deferred:** Minimum Secret Cup LV15-versus-LV17 threshold, Speed Cup time-group assignment, exact HD score equivalence and spendable Medal payout matrix.

**Why:** The LV17 plan reaches the goal without deciding the minimum; score-improvement tactics and visible results avoid needing a predictive medal/time optimizer. No missing score group or fixed earnings estimate is invented.

**Reopen:** Secret Cup remains locked after the conservative requirements, a rank guide demonstrably fails, or a medal-budget optimizer is approved.

## DDD-018

**Goal:** Use toys/Reality Shifts, train affinity and earn their score awards.

**Current route:** 24 item acquisition entries and HD action/scoring references are present. The toy achievements now point to the appropriate acquisition and control references; current Steam prompts handle remapped inputs. Treat-color and two-step interaction routes provide workable training.

**Deferred:** Every Steam input device binding, breed-optimal training/size formula and exact toy efficiency model.

**Why:** The goal is successful actions and threshold scores, not a globally optimal breed-by-breed training schedule. Known toy effects and prompt-based controls are sufficient without asserting unknown numerical efficiencies.

**Reopen:** A concrete toy/action cannot be executed or a score threshold cannot be reached with current guidance.

## DDD-019

**Goal:** Defeat Lord Kyroo and obtain the recipe/finishing-character reward.

**Current route:** The encounter sequence, 70-second escape, retained damage, mid-room-exit reset and finishing-character reward are documented. A conservative no-reload chain is now explicit.

**Reopen:** A player needs to resume an interrupted hunt or encounters a reproducible reset inconsistent with the current advice.

## DDD-020

**Goal:** Own every eligible Keyblade on both characters.

**Current route:** All 15 types and 13-per-character eligibility are retained. HD sources support Sweet Dreams delivery to the clearing character and a full Secret Cup replay for the other; instructions require separate ownership checks.

## DDD-021

**Goal:** Earn the 18 in-game trophies, including Dream Pleaser and Daring Diver.

**Current route:** Daring Diver now directs players to exceed 7,500,000 in the sum of retained world/character high scores, based on an explicit HD guide, rather than a conservative either-counter guess. Dream Pleaser explicitly calls for one instance of each of 54 breeds at affinity level9; retained-instance advice remains a precaution, not a release/recovery rule.

**Deferred:** Dream Pleaser released-instance/NG+ retention and internal implementation; an actual already-released or failed-award case must be investigated rather than declared solved by retaining future instances.

**Why:** The ordinary trophy actions are now concrete and Daring Diver’s useful aggregation question was researched, not deferred. Only unneeded internal persistence precision is deferred. Existing-save recovery is not claimed covered.

**Reopen:** A player has released or transferred trained Spirits and needs to establish retained credit, or the award fails after the documented normal requirements.

## DDD-022

**Goal:** Track and earn the 54 Steam DDD achievements.

**Current route:** All 54 public names/requirements and observed native Steam keys are mapped; manual stable checklist IDs are preserved. Difficulty constraints such as Ability Ace remain visible.

**Deferred:** Hidden Steam counter implementation and PSN/Xbox/Epic native identifier schemas.

**Why:** The app tracks manually and does not read native saves or other-platform achievements. Alternate-platform IDs and implementation flags do not help the present Steam checklist unless a concrete award problem occurs.

**Reopen:** A reproducible Steam unlock issue needs diagnosis or a separately approved platform integration needs native IDs.

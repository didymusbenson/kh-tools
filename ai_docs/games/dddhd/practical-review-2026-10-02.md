## Remaining actionable research

### DDD-025 — wrong-answer recovery

The normal unlock guide is sufficient before the finale, and retaining a pre-finale save provides an ordinary retry of that known route. It does not answer the player who already saved incorrect answers and has no backup. Fresh searches again found pre-HD 2012–2013 discussions, modern forum categories retroactively labelled HD, cross-platform mirrored answers, and unlabelled later player posts. None certifies a Steam/HD clear-save recovery. The [unlock criteria](https://www.khwiki.com/Another_Guardian_of_Light) explicitly support credits-letter replay, not answer-flag repair.

**Bounded next evidence:** one edition-labelled successful HD recovery procedure, or trustworthy event documentation showing recovery is impossible. Do not expand this into an exhaustive matrix of all answer, trophy, letter, boss and save combinations. Do not tell players the ending is permanently missable or promise a Theater-only repair.

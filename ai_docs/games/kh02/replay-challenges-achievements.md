# 0.2 replay, optional challenges and platform achievements

Research date: 2026-09-18. This is a separate goals track. None of these encounter results, levels or story milestones contributes to area collection percentages.

## Availability and replay

A first clear unlocks Critical and the late objective set. Objectives 36–50 become available on revisiting their area after clear, or from the start of NG+; 42 additionally depends on all Zodiac relics. A fresh first run cannot complete all 51. Preserve a clear-data lineage rather than assuming unrelated saves share objective rewards. [KHWiki objective availability](https://www.khwiki.com/Wardrobe_(KH0.2)), [difficulty rules](https://www.khwiki.com/Difficulty_Level#Kingdom_Hearts_0.2_Birth_by_Sleep_-A_fragmentary_passage-).

PSU recommends NG+ for objectives **36, 41, 47, 50, 51**. Its routes distinguish the replay-only story encounter tasks from post-clear exploration. The rail challenge #46 can be revisited. Keep a pre-encounter save for retries; document platform-specific reload controls only after validation. [PSU objectives](https://www.psu.com/news/kingdom-hearts-0-2-objectives-guide-complete-all-challenges/).

**Practical planning route:** finish an initial run, collect post-clear exploration rewards and complete difficult optional fights on the preferred lower difficulty, then seed a Critical run with that progress. Complete #36/#41/#47/#50 while their story encounters are live and #51 at the final Demon Tide. This is a suggested itinerary, not a forced completion order or a new story checklist. Do not perform or require a level-80 grind twice by accidentally conflating run-local level with earned #09 completion.

## Zodiac Mirror challenge

Collect the 12 relics, return to the central Zodiac circle in The World Within, then enter the new mirror. Five rounds lead to the Tiara reward through objective 42. [PSU five-round breakdown](https://www.psu.com/news/kingdom-hearts-0-2-how-to-complete-objective-42-in-the-mirror/).

| Round | Encounter |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Darkside; Flutterings, Shadows, Neoshadows |
| 2 | Flame, Earth and Water Cores |
| 3 | Shadows, Neoshadows, Flutterings |
| 4 | Demon Tower and Shadows |
| 5 | Enhanced Phantom Aqua |

Exiting to prepare restarts the sequence from round one. Store a challenge-clear state and optional round notes; neither duplicates the relics' collection state. Do not introduce a mandatory no-damage condition for this challenge: objective 41 targets the third story Phantom instead.

The enhanced Phantom has red-aura attacks that require evasion, and limited openings after attack strings. Keep defensive strategy version-aware: Critical changes Aqua's survival and healing behavior. The gameplay wiki labels boss stats/strategy sections as needing improvement, so its numeric stats are not production-certified. [Phantom Aqua gameplay](https://www.khwiki.com/Game:Phantom_Aqua).

## Difficulty affects strategy, not the collectible denominator

KHWiki's 0.2 table reports these differences from Standard: Beginner increases Aqua damage by 50% and halves incoming damage; Proud halves Aqua damage while incoming damage stays normal; Critical increases Aqua damage by 50%, doubles incoming damage, halves HP/MP values with rounding, removes restorative enemy/destructible prizes, permits Curaga interruption and removes certain protective effects. These are community facts awaiting current-build confirmation. [0.2 difficulty table](https://www.khwiki.com/Difficulty_Level#Kingdom_Hearts_0.2_Birth_by_Sleep_-A_fragmentary_passage-).

Objective 51 specifically needs Critical. The platform difficulty achievement allows **Proud or Critical**; satisfying the latter on Proud does not satisfy #51. Do not import BBS Critical rules or KH3 Critical abilities into this game.

## Deferred research and reasoning

### KH02-001

- **Player goal:** Complete Master of Lightning and receive Mystic Pauldron.
- **Already sufficient / integrated now:** Objective and wardrobe instructions now tell players to finish Heartless with Thundaga, budget for 50 defeats, and stop as soon as the game awards completion. The 30/50 conflict remains explicit.
- **Deferred precision:** Whether the current Steam threshold is exactly 30 or 50 and its underlying event counter.
- **Why defer:** The conservative route satisfies both published counts and supplies an observable stopping condition. Resolving the minimum would save at most the difference in kills, not unlock a currently blocked activity. This is a pragmatic deferral, not a factual resolution of the contradiction.
- **Reopen when:** The objective does not award after the conservative lightning-final-blow route, or precise minimal-kill routing becomes necessary.

### KH02-005

- **Player goal:** Open all Forest chests for the area objective and Treasure Hunter.
- **Already sufficient / integrated now:** The save-point ivy chest is physically located irrespective of Ether/Mega-Ether naming. The northern record now also offers Destiny Islands chest-8 black-thorn dead-end search directions, with explicit uncertainty about its cross-source join and Steam Hi-Potion label. All ten ordinary Forest route records remain available.
- **Deferred precision:** Definitive modern item-popup identity for save-point Ether/Mega-Ether and independent binding of northern Potion versus northwest-thicket Hi-Potion.
- **Why defer:** For opening chests, the physical alcove/search route is actionable without deciding the consumable label. The candidate join is not silently certified, no duplicate chest is added, and the 55-find denominator stays unchanged. Exact content naming is optional precision unless it prevents matching a remaining chest.
- **Reopen when:** A player has an unlocatable Forest chest or the candidate search route duplicates another record; then treat identity as a concrete blocker.

### KH02-010

- **Player goal:** Keep earned collection progress while completing post-clear objectives and moving to Critical NG+.
- **Already sufficient / integrated now:** Replay guidance now recommends all seven gems in one visit, all four memories and the complete chest sweep on the retained clear save, keeping that save separate before NG+. Known completed-objective/wardrobe and Zodiac retention remains documented.
- **Deferred precision:** Per-asset partial gem/memory copy/reset behavior and ordinary-chest flags across Steam NG+ lineage.
- **Why defer:** Completing each set on the retained source save avoids relying on unproved transfer behavior. The app is a manual checklist, not a save-file importer; reverse-engineering every copied bit would not improve this safe route.
- **Reopen when:** A concrete recovery case cannot be completed from its retained source save, or a save-inspection feature is requested.

### KH02-011

- **Deferred precision:** Zodiac substitution for objective 31, minimal active-versus-Finish predicate for 50, and pre-unlock partial counter retroactivity.
- **Why defer:** The supported live-story route works without relying on rematch substitution or retroactive progress. Extra trigger archaeology could remove a repeat or relax an input, but is not needed to complete the objective using the stated conservative method.

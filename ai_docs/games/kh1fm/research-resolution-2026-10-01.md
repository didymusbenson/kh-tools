### KH1-015–020: mapping conflicts and provenance

- **015:** [KHGuides awakening](https://www.khguides.com/kh/awakening/) says “average” without rounding boundaries; [ElectroSpecter FAQ](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps4/241159-kingdom-hearts-all-in-one-package/faqs/19404) contains a legacy 27-case table; [HD forum](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/boards/684080-kingdom-hearts-hd-15-remix/67819598) suggests majority. The full table is asymmetric, including all-middle-Wakka combinations yielding Midday, so cannot be equated to a simple majority. Platform-indexing an old FAQ under PS4 does not prove its table was re-tested on Steam. The temporary majority interpretation was rejected before final generation.
- **016:** [Steam fresh-game Theater discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2552430/discussions/0/6364229748853049069/) establishes separate collection-menu access independently of a progressed save. The discussion speaks of general cutscenes, not an enumerated secret-movie list. Existing difficulty/save predicates remain supported; specific fresh-profile availability of Another Side and Deep Dive still needs explicit evidence.
- **017:** [KHGuides Gummis](https://www.khguides.com/kh/collectibles/gummis/) gives the entire 28-row enemy blueprint table and a shared 10% chance. Every row and its route count/phase markers was transcribed, not only a sample. Individual ship pages were also checked; their part-drop tables are separate from blueprint probability. “↔” is explicitly bidirectional. Superscript 1 means after the first Hollow Bastion story episode, not an invented one-way condition.
- **018:** Dedicated pages checked for [Critical Plus](https://www.khwiki.com/Critical_Plus), [Treasure Magnet](https://www.khwiki.com/Treasure_Magnet), [MP Rage](https://www.khwiki.com/MP_Rage), [Berserk](https://www.khwiki.com/Berserk_(ability)), [Jackpot](https://www.khwiki.com/Jackpot), [Tech Boost](https://www.khwiki.com/Tech_Boost), [Cheer](https://www.khwiki.com/Cheer), [Second Wind](https://www.khwiki.com/Second_Wind). Effects distinguish charge from direct MP, prize quantity from item drops and deflection Tech from special-enemy-hit Tech. [MP Haste](https://www.khwiki.com/MP_Haste) is qualitative for KH1; [HD experiment](https://www.reddit.com/r/KingdomHearts/comments/1koased/how_mp_recharge_works/) explicitly disputes +15 with +12. [Ultimania-derived Magic FAQ](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps3/684080-kingdom-hearts-hd-15-remix/faqs/19743) is a legacy lead. No KH2 MP cooldown percentage is imported.
- **019:** [Public global-percentage API](https://api.steampowered.com/ISteamUserStats/GetGlobalAchievementPercentagesForApp/v2/?gameid=2552430&format=json) returned HTTP 200 and ACH_001–197. [Community grouping](https://github.com/joamjoamjoam/SteamAchievementViewer) identifies KH1's first 55. English Steam global HTML has 197 name/description/percentage/opaque-icon rows, with no ACH_ key, `g_rgAchievements` or schema object. `GetSchemaForGame/v2/?appid=2552430` returned HTTP 400 without a key; direct SteamDB returned 403, and search rendering only yielded metadata/loading. [steam-key-provenance.json](steam-key-provenance.json) saves the key roster, response hash and exact outcomes. Rounded percentages and page order are not used to infer mappings.
- **020:** [legacy-value-crosswalk.json](legacy-value-crosswalk.json), regenerated by `tools/content/audit-kh1-legacy.py`, contains all 824 parsed rows, source file hashes, explicit current IDs/group contexts and 1,107 field comparisons. Narrative biography/format rows are excluded explicitly; old hand-maintained ingredient totals are rejected in favor of recipe calculation. Trinity color/world groups and legacy magic-tier groups are intentionally not guessed one-to-one matches. Full semantic acceptance/rejection of every prose clause remains a residual, rather than falsely calling numeric equality a full value audit. [SteamDB metadata](https://steamdb.info/app/2552430/) reports a 2024 Steam Deck tested build 14690043; that is not this pass's examined executable and must not be promoted to a 2026 researched build. No installed binary/depot manifest was available. FM/Steam scope and checked dates are established; precise binary-build provenance is not.

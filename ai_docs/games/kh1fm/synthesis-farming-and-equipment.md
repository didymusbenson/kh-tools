Pink Agaricus has reward checkpoints: 40/50/60 hits offer 10% Serenity Power rolls; 70/80/90 offer 20%; 100 grants a guaranteed one and Prime Cap. Allies' hits can reduce the tally. The 1.5+2.5 release corrected the PS3 reaction-input issue that inadvertently called party attacks; do not prescribe that older workaround to all modern players. [Hit rewards](https://www.khwiki.com/Pink_Agaricus), [modern collection changes](https://www.khwiki.com/Kingdom_Hearts_HD_1.5_%2B_2.5_ReMIX)

The Stormy Stone chance is 35% on the seventh qualifying Neoshadow; it is not an ordinary drop from each enemy. [Material condition](https://www.khwiki.com/Gale)

## Drop modifiers and reset behavior

Lucky Strike's KH1 multiplier is `1 + 0.5 × active equipped copies`; three copies yield 2.5× the base rate. It costs 2 AP in Final Mix. Model the applicable active party, rather than every owned ability. Do not blindly apply the ordinary-drop formula to scripted rewards. [Lucky Strike mechanics](https://www.khwiki.com/Lucky_Strike)

Encounter Plus costs 1 AP and is earned after 15 distinct synthesized products. Ordinary enemies reset after one room change rather than two; rare Final Mix and Mushroom encounters use two area changes rather than leaving/re-entering the world. Separate “reset eligible” from “guaranteed special spawn.” [Encounter Plus](https://www.khwiki.com/Encounter_Plus)

## Mushroom challenges and rare rewards

- **White Mushroom:** match the charade three times. Repeating the same spell for all three yields that spell's Arts item. Collect all seven Arts for Dream Shield. Wrong spells, damage or delays can end the attempt. Record Fire/Blizzard/Thunder/Cure/Gravity/Stop/Aero Arts individually. [White Mushroom](https://www.khwiki.com/White_Mushroom), [Dream Shield](https://www.khwiki.com/Dream_Shield)
- **Rare Truffle:** keep it airborne; dropping it ends the sequence. Track 10/50/100-hit milestones, Shiitake Rank at 50 and Matsutake Rank at 100. Aerora/Aeroga positioning or Neverland flight can help sustain the sequence. Keep Final Mix rewards separate from the original game's table. [Rare Truffle](https://www.khwiki.com/Rare_Truffle)
- **Black Fungus:** hostile, periodically invulnerable; a qualifying combo finish can produce Mystery Goo/Mystery Mold. Preserve the finisher condition rather than treating the displayed rate as an unconditional kill roll. [Black Fungus](https://www.khwiki.com/Black_Fungus)
- **Pink Agaricus:** Prime Cap is the 100-hit challenge reward, distinct from farming enough Serenity Power for recipes. [Pink Agaricus](https://www.khwiki.com/Pink_Agaricus)

These optional collectible items must have explicit goal membership. They must not silently become requirements for a platform trophy whose actual condition is narrower.

## Accessories, stats and outstanding verification

The catalog also needs every accessory, consumable, permanent stat item, spell Arts/rank item and progression key item, with its own acquisition routes. Accessory sources include shops, world chests, boss rewards and synthesis. The recipe table covers synthesized accessories; world and shop inventories cover additional ones. Import KH1FM Strength/MP/AP/HP/Defense/resistances, weapon reach/recoil/critical properties and special effects from the linked item pages, not their KH2/KH3 sections.

**Source conflict KH1-C01:** the [Energy Bangle recipe page](https://www.khwiki.com/Energy_Bangle) lists **2 Spirit Shards + 1 Bright Shard**, while the [Spirit material usage summary](https://www.khwiki.com/Spirit_Shard) lists **1 Spirit Shard** for that product. The recipe reference provisionally uses the dedicated recipe page. Its calculated direct Spirit Shard total is therefore 13. Resolve against the modern in-game recipe before marking the recipe set verified; if 1 is correct, that direct total becomes 12. The discrepancy remains visible rather than silently “fixed.”

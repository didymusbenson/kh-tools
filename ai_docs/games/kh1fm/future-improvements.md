## KH1-003 — Restricted-run achievement details

**Disposition: practical needs met; further research deferred by user decision on 2026-10-02.**

The journal already includes manual achievement tracking and a restricted-run planning guide (`kh1fm-guide-restricted-run-planning`) for Unchanging Armor, Undefeated and Speedster. Existing advice covers avoiding manual equipment swaps, reloading a clean save instead of continuing, budgeting the under-15-hour run, and checking achievement awards after the ending. That is sufficient for the current feature.

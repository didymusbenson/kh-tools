## Decisions and evidence

- **Level cells:** blank means no assertion, never “no reward.” Named ability/stat labels are accepted only as subsets of the full current reward. Historical `Hurrican Blast`, `Tech Plus` and `MP Up` normalize to Hurricane Blast, Tech Boost and Max MP; amounts and shared gains remain explicit in the current record. Rows 76–83 have eight incorrect shared-stat labels and are rejected in favor of the corresponding `kh1fm-guide-sora-level-76` through `-83` fields. The current records cite [KHGuides' level table](https://www.khguides.com/kh/awakening/dream-weapon-selection/) and the [documented vanilla Sora stat table](https://github.com/gaithern/KH1FM-RANDOMIZER/blob/main/Documentation/KH1FM%20Documentation%20-%20Battle%20Table%20Sora%20Level%20Up%20Stats.csv). This uses the repository's documented vanilla data, not randomized output.
- **Enemy drops:** split every ordinary comma-separated item list and every Rare Truffle tier into individual item claims. Accept means available under the current field's stated conditions/rates, not guaranteed or exhaustive. Reject Angel Star's Mythril, Invisible's Orichalcum, Large Body's copied Gale/Orichalcum, and Sheltering Zone's copied Hi-Potion/Mega-Potion/Bright Gem. Current enemy reward tables already omit them. Generic Behemoth rewards/worlds are superseded by explicit Arch Behemoth variant context, not declared false for every Behemoth. Gale Stone is superseded by the current Stormy Stone identity. Canonical entries and their per-enemy KHWiki citations are embedded in the artifact.
- **Special encounters:** Rare Truffle's ten-juggle Elixir is rejected; its other tier/item pairs are accepted only with current probabilities. White Mushroom's seven gesture/spell pairs match; parenthetical shard/gem/goo lists are superseded by the mixed-spell versus three-identical-spell conditions, not interpreted as a guaranteed bundle. Battleship 300 HP applies to the body, not every part. Zero ordinary EXP for Gigas Shadow/Rare Truffle does not mean zero Tech Points. See [Rare Truffle](https://www.khwiki.com/Rare_Truffle), [White Mushroom](https://www.khwiki.com/White_Mushroom), and the corresponding catalog entries.
- **Enemy worlds:** each named world is compared separately; accepted subsets do not assert completeness. Normalize “The End of the World” and the joined “AtlanticaHalloween Town” cells explicitly. Rare Truffle's End of the World is rejected for the current Final Mix encounter list; generic Behemoth Hollow Bastion is not attributed to Arch Behemoth.
- **Damage rules:** absorption is accepted literally. Phase-qualified leading multipliers are accepted only in their normal/pre-rescue context and therefore supersede an unconditional scalar. Bandit's post-rescue values cannot stand for pre-rescue; Defender/Wizard incorrect baseline values are rejected. Black Fungus fixed one damage and Rare Truffle fixed two damage are not x1 multipliers. White Mushroom ordinary and stationary-trigger exceptions remain distinct.
- **Trinities:** all 46 marks have explicit one-to-one IDs, including the Olympus/Olympus Coliseum alias and reordered Deep Jungle/Monstro/Traverse marks. Locations and rewards are assessed separately. Reject legacy Climbing Trees puppies (current Thundara-G), Bazaar Mega-Elixir (Mega-Ether), Silent Chamber Thunder-G (Thundara-G), Storage Power Up (AP Up), and Merlin-crates Power Up (AP Up). Reject green Neverland Hold (Cabin), original Oogie entrance-room red mark (Manor Ruins arch), and red Hollow Bastion Grand Hall (Entrance Hall upper statue). Broad rewards and spelling aliases do not claim identical quantities. All current per-mark IDs, precise routes, rewards and [Trinity Mark source](https://www.khwiki.com/Trinity_Mark) citations are included in the artifact.

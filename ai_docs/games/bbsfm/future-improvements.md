# BBS Final Mix — deferred precision

Updated 2026-10-03. These are the 16 current deferred families. The former 19-family list is superseded: practical gaps were corrected or moved to the five-family open set. Deferral does not change factual evidence status. See the [all-family critical re-audit](critical-reaudit-2026-10-03.md) for exact open rows and separate limitations.

## BBS-001 — Precise collectible directions

**Player goal:** Find the remaining chest or sticker and complete the character collection.

**Sufficient current guidance:** All 374 main chest rows and eight Secret Episode rows retain approach guidance. The 39 specifically weak chest rows have inspected locators, including paired-chest directions. The complete 60-row sticker census now has 60 useful within-room locators after correcting 26 weak notes. All 60 album placements remain separate and intact.

**Deferred details:** Uniform save-point-to-pickup scripts, exact minimum movement and earliest access, and single-chest reward assignment where opening the documented adjacent pair suffices.

**Reason:** After actually correcting the 39 weak chest rows and 26 weak sticker notes, current landmarks identify the collection target. A complete repeat walkthrough adds precision without changing that action. Mere existence of 60 notes was not accepted as evidence of sufficiency.

**Reopen when:** A named collectible remains unlocatable with its supplied landmark/approach, or a requested route needs a specific earlier or restricted movement solution.

## Minigames and reward routes

[Rumble Racing](https://www.khwiki.com/Rumble_Racing): four courses. Arena five-lap thresholds are Country Chase 2:30, Disney Drive 5:00, Grand Spree 5:00 and Castle Circuit 5:30. Winning a race, a top-three reward and meeting an Arena time are distinct predicates. Grand Spree needs top-three finishes in Country Chase and Disney Drive; Castle Circuit needs first place in Grand Spree. Victory Line is Castle Circuit first place.

[Ice Cream Beat](https://www.khwiki.com/Ice_Cream_Beat): each song's Beginner “Cool” unlocks Master; Hand in Hand needs “Cool” on the other four Master songs. Rating uses the score column, not the height-added total. Final Mix Master Fantastic thresholds/rewards:

| Song | Threshold | First reward |
|---|---:|---|
| It's a Small World | 2,488 | Elixir |
| Blast Away! -Gummi Ship II- | 3,270 | Blizzara |
| Dessert Paradise | 2,500 | Frozen Fortune |
| Destiny Islands | 2,958 | Chaos Crystal |
| Hand in Hand | 3,260 | Blizzaga |

Hand in Hand Beginner Fantastic (1,144) grants Freeze Raid. Keep repeated-play prizes separate from first rewards.

[Fruitball](https://www.khwiki.com/Fruitball): repeatable opponents are Bruisers, Chip & Dale and Pete (Captain Dark for Terra; Captain Justice for Ventus/Aqua). Aqua's story Shoegazers match is separate. First Chip & Dale victory grants Magnera; Pete grants Chaos Snake. Capture all three repeatable results per character.

[Command Board](https://www.khwiki.com/Command_Board): seven boards are Keyblade, Royal, Spaceship, Toon, Skull, Hunny Pot and Secret. Hunny Pot comes from Merlin's book; Secret requires a win on each other board. Menu and Arena sessions must be distinguished: acquisition panels and opponent-command purchases are not equivalent across modes. Bonus Shotlocks may be replaced while currently owned. Pete's special panel event unlocks his D-Link. Full per-board panel inventories and mode exceptions remain open.

## Finish trees and optional bosses

[acquisition-tables.json](acquisition-tables.json) normalizes 30 listed finish-command definitions into character-specific unlock predicates: required equipped parent, metric, threshold and style trigger. For example, Surprise! 1 requires 1,400 collected munny while Gold Rush is equipped; Heal Strike requires surviving lethal hits five times with the character's level-three attack parent equipped. These are branch conditions, not ordinary character-level unlocks. [Finish commands](https://www.khwiki.com/Finish_command_(KHBBS)). D-Link, Style and Illusion finishers are separate categories and still need complete acquisition/usage normalization.

The optional boss set includes Vanitas Remnant (Badlands), Unknown (Land of Departure), Armor of Eraqus (Light's Lessons), No Heart (Peering into Darkness) and Monstro (Monster of the Sea); the Secret Episode has Dark Hide. Retain distinct clear/reward records per character or episode. Boss mechanics, exact access prerequisites and recovery strategies remain research tasks. [Final Episode](https://www.khwiki.com/Final_Episode), [BBSFM additions](https://www.khwiki.com/Kingdom_Hearts_Birth_by_Sleep_Final_Mix).

## Final and Secret Episode unlocks

Final Episode requires all three main stories and all Xehanort report acquisitions. Keep the three main clear files and the Aqua-derived Final/Secret saves distinct. Aqua's Final Episode carries over her completed-save progress; Radiant Garden becomes its boss entry and Land of Departure is unavailable in that episode. [Final Episode](https://www.khwiki.com/Final_Episode).

For Final Mix, the inspected [Blank Points](https://www.khwiki.com/Blank_Points) / [Secret Episode](https://www.khwiki.com/A_Fragmentary_Passage) references give:

| Difficulty | Additional requirements after Final Episode |
|---|---|
| Beginner | All three albums complete; Keyslinger, In the Munny, Power Walker |
| Standard | All three albums complete; Keyslinger |
| Proud | All three albums complete |
| Critical | None listed |

Use 140-point completed albums for the plan; verify mixed-difficulty and already-cleared-save behavior before certifying the unlock instructions. These are reference prerequisites, not a progress-gate tracker. Keyslinger (9,999 Unversed), In the Munny (33,333 munny), Power Walker (99,999 steps) are **in-game Trinity trophies**, not platform achievements. The BBS Secret Episode named A Fragmentary Passage is not the separate **0.2** game. Never send users to 0.2 to satisfy BBS Secret Episode completion.

## Separate achievement overlay

The public [Steam achievement list](https://steamcommunity.com/stats/2552430/achievements) contains **197 collection-wide entries** and duplicate names across games. It is not a BBS total; hidden descriptions are empty in its public view. The [community BBSFM roster](https://www.khwiki.com/Trophies#Kingdom_Hearts_Birth_by_Sleep_Final_Mix) contains **46** entries including the PlayStation platinum; platform identity and current build applicability remain unverified.

Track scoped requirements such as three Adventurer Reports goals, three Warrior finish-tree goals, all stickers, nine mission ranks, minigame wins, ice cream manufacture, Shotlock 50 uses, Pete D-Link 10 uses and Rhythm Mixer 20 activations. The separate overlay may require broader Reports categories, including narrative entries; ordinary world collection does not. Do not require Reports story/character completion to obtain a world collectible checkmark or world collection 100%.

Open gates: exact platform roster/IDs, hidden predicates, whether a goal needs one or all characters, modern difficulty/save aggregation, and the claimed Collector glitch's affected builds. Do not prescribe deleting system data from an unverified community note. PSP trophy-transfer bonuses are excluded from modern acquisition planning.

## Method and accounting

The question is whether the missing fact changes progress, acquisition, farming or achievement work. A room-only note is not a locator. A shop price without a needed gate is not complete access guidance. Known inputs and completion actions must be supplied before damage/probability precision is deferred. Source-backed walkthrough synthesis is labeled; neither unavailable retail binaries nor a required user playthrough is a blanket stopping condition.

- Original evidence inventory is unchanged: 12 closed, 17 partial, 4 researched-open, 2 provenance, 1 implementation, 1 visual and 1 excluded.
- The 21 nonclosed factual families now comprise 16 optional-precision deferrals and 5 practical open families.
- All 38 findings have flat playerGoal, sufficientGuidance, deferredDetails, deferralReason, reopenWhen and practicalReviewReport fields in the [ledger](research-dispositions-2026-10-01.json). Open families additionally enumerate openRecordIds.
- Data Jiminy, atomic inventory transactions, physical-device acceptance and production artwork were not completed by this research pass.

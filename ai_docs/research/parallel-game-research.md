## Lessons carried forward from KH1

- KHWiki's `Game:World` pages and individual item/recipe/enemy pages were useful **community references**, not primary evidence. Select the correct game's section and FM/HD edition markers; do not merge sequels or original-release columns.
- Use official publisher/store/platform pages for release contents and published achievement requirements where available.
- Inspect actual source ranges, not merely sheet names; record coverage and partial reads.
- Recalculate recipe totals from ingredients and record conflicts explicitly.
- Distinguish achievement thresholds from exhaustive collectible inventories.
- Prioritize exact text locations, access conditions and acquisition mechanics over narrative Journal replication.
- Derive compact/detail views and counts from common stable records. Preserve character and DLC boundaries.
- Do not report a linked source as imported or in-game-verified data. Leave specific unresolved facts visible.
- Every specified game and feature remains MVP unless explicitly deferred; missing production image assets are the existing exception.

# Ars Arcanum research: final decisions and deferrals

Completed October 3, 2026. All **65 starting residual research families** across KH2, BBS, DDD, KH0.2 and KH3 were reviewed against the app and their evidence: **2 resolved, 52 with optional residual details deferred, and 11 still open**. The full five-game inventory is **156 families: 82 resolved, 52 deferred, 11 open, and 11 separate limitations**. KH1’s completed decisions and the separately owned CoM work were preserved.

## Why there are still many deferrals

The denominator is a backlog of residual unknowns after earlier research, not 65 untouched features. Some broad families received substantial practical fixes and still retain optional precision. Their final label describes the remaining question, not the amount of research performed. A threshold-equality question and an entire farming family each count once, so the count is not a measure of effort or feature completeness.

**The initial 61-deferral / four-open result was too broad and is superseded.** The stricter review repaired missing routes and actions, resolved two whole families, and reopened useful questions even where an alternative route exists. A safer future playthrough is not evidence that an already affected save can be repaired. [Correction record](practical-review-reassessment-2026-10-02.md).

Examples of the final boundary: exact Mushroom AI internals do not improve an already supplied challenge/reward route; minimum Ignite Lv1 versus a supported Lv3 input is an optional shortcut; unknown medal odds do not excuse missing minigame instructions, which are now supplied. Aura Lion’s disputed board action and recovery of an affected NG+ achievement remain open.

## Practical changes made

- **KH2:** checked all 131 equipment acquisition records, the three-crown Mineshaft sweep and Mushroom challenge/reward instructions. Retained the real Dark Anklet cutoff; did not invent shop flags or hidden battle stats.
- **BBS:** repaired 22 empty command-acquisition lists, removed 13 inapplicable legacy/temporary acquisition claims and qualified 236 menu-Command-Board routes. Added precise chest routes, farm instructions, finisher-hit/steering actions, board-footnote explanations and useful Arena/shop conditions. The BBS report separates the audited pickup census from finite remaining disputes.
- **DDD:** fixed Jestabocky’s discarded one-sided connector rather than treating disconnected app data as an in-game mystery. Added named-recipe versus custom-mode instructions, 22 portal landmarks, scarce-material repeat farming, seven concrete bonus-challenge locations, a ★-rank Golden Egg recipe, all five missing-disposition detours and completion actions. Resolved Daring Diver’s recorded-high-score aggregation.
- **KH0.2:** established the northern Forest chest’s physical locator; only two consumable-label disputes remain. Added Lightning completion actions, visible-objective timing and the reported post-clear Phantom boss-rush recovery for objective 31. Kept the real retained-Zodiac NG+ award/recovery question open.
- **KH3:** resolved Forest Clasp’s cutoff, supplied all seven Flan and five score-minigame methods, audited acquisition actions for all 157 equipment records, made 33 known aliases searchable, and added naval/crab/boarding guidance. Existing work supplies all 33 Gummi battle approaches and routes for all 60 synthesis materials.

Stable existing entry/recipe IDs and source uncertainty were preserved. No source guide or parser validation is represented as independent Steam gameplay or installed-binary certification.

## Accepted direction

- Collectible/acquisition compendium; crafting is first-class. Ordinary narrative/biography manifests are not release blockers.
- Steam HD baseline for this user. 2.8 is a collection, not a standalone game. Announced 2026-10-08 ports are unverified/unreleased at audit.
- Fully spoilerific: no warnings, hidden content or reveal controls.
- No Available Now/progress-gate tracking/filter; preserve concise text acquisition prerequisites.
- Optional opt-in material inventory, with owned/required (x/y) on recipe ingredients.
- React offline PWA, local persistent progress/backup, bundled local SLM and DDD Data Jiminy pack remain MVP.
- Compact marks and expanded location rows share stable records and synchronized persisted state. World percentages count collectibles only; filtering never changes denominators.
- Only missing production screenshot/map image assets are deferred. Text guidance and media support/testing remain MVP.
- Validate sources/calculations and app behavior; no mandatory manual gameplay/playthrough gate. Initial functional acceptance: Apple browser/iPhone/iPad; Android follow-up.
